Process

How it works

1

Sign up as Student, Mentor, or Admin

Create your account in seconds. The platform shows the right dashboard automatically — practice tools for students, review tools for mentors, management tools for admins.

2

Upload resume — get instant AI feedback

Drag and drop your PDF. Gemini Vision reads it visually (works on scanned PDFs too) and returns an overall score, ATS rating, section feedback, and missing keywords in seconds.

3

Practice company-specific questions

Pick a company, take the assessment, see your score instantly. History is tracked with visual charts so you can spot weak areas and measure improvement.

4

Get mentor feedback & land the job

Your assigned mentor reviews your resume, provides targeted feedback (AI-drafted in one click), and you iterate. The admin tracks placements and manages the whole ecosystem.
