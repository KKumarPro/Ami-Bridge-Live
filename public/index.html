Features

Everything you need to get placed

A complete placement preparation toolkit powered by AI — built for Indian engineering colleges.

AI Resume Analysis

Upload your PDF — our AI gives an ATS score, highlights missing keywords, and provides section-by-section feedback using Groq LLM for text and Gemini Vision for scanned resumes.

Groq LLM Gemini Vision

Local Internships

Discover offline internship opportunities from local companies near you — both paid and unpaid. Mentors and admins post listings; students apply directly via email.

Paid & Unpaid Local Companies

Interview Practice

Practice company-specific interview questions curated by your institution. Track your attempt history and see improvement over time.

Company Q&A History Tracking

Mentor Feedback

Every student is assigned a dedicated mentor who reviews their resume, gives detailed feedback, and guides them through the placement process.

1-on-1 Guidance AI-Assisted

Bulk Registration

Admins and mentors can register entire batches of students via CSV upload — saving hours of manual effort for placement coordinators.

CSV Upload Batch Processing

Performance Analytics

Track resume scores, interview attempt history, and placement readiness across your entire student cohort with a live analytics dashboard.

Live Charts ATS Tracking

How it works

From signup to placement

Three parallel journeys. One shared goal.

Student

  • 1
    Create account Sign up as a student and complete your profile.
  • 2
    Upload resume AI instantly scores your PDF and flags improvements.
  • 3
    Practice interviews Work through company-specific question banks.
  • 4
    Apply to internships Browse and apply to local listings from companies near you.
  • 5
    Get placed Armed with a great resume and mentor backing.

Mentor

  • 1
    Join platform Register as a mentor and get assigned students by an admin.
  • 2
    Review resumes Access student PDFs with AI OCR — even scanned files.
  • 3
    Give feedback Use the AI draft assistant to write detailed, personalised feedback.
  • 4
    Post internships Add local company listings for your students to apply to.
  • 5
    Track progress Monitor each student's score improvement and readiness.

Admin

  • 1
    Set up platform Configure your institution's companies and Q&A bank.
  • 2
    Register students Bulk-upload entire batches via CSV in seconds.
  • 3
    Assign mentors Pair each student with the most suitable mentor.
  • 4
    Monitor analytics Track placement readiness across the full cohort.
  • 5
    Drive outcomes Use data to intervene early and maximise placements.
