Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ami-Bridge ("Platform", "we", "our", or "us"). Ami-Bridge is an intelligent campus placement and career readiness platform designed to facilitate student recruitment, AI-powered resume analysis, mock interview preparation, and mentor-student collaboration.

We take data privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we handle the collection, use, processing, and protection of personal data belonging to students, mentors, company recruiters, and academic administrators.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide career and placement intelligence services, we collect:

  • Account Details: Full name, university email address, contact number, enrollment ID, and graduation year.
  • Academic & Portfolio Data: CGPA, degree program, technical skills, project links, and professional profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn).
  • Resume & Application Files: Resumes and CVs uploaded for automated parsing, scoring, and placement discovery.
  • Interview & Performance Insights: Responses, feedback notes, and performance evaluations from mock interview sessions.
  • System Log Data: Device browser, IP address, and platform usage metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for platform functionalities:

  • Generating AI-powered resume scoring, skill-gap analysis, and feedback recommendations.
  • Matching student profiles with active internship and placement opportunities.
  • Facilitating mentor guidance, interview scheduling, and activity tracking.
  • Providing institutional placement coordinators with aggregated hiring statistics.

AI Processing Confidentiality: Resumes and responses evaluated by integrated AI services are processed securely via encrypted APIs and are never sold or used for external public model training.

4. Information Sharing & Access Controls

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is shared strictly within authorized placement operations:

  • University Placement Coordinators: For academic tracking and campus drive management.
  • Verified Recruiters: Profile and resume access when students apply for participating companies.
  • Assigned Mentors: For providing personalized reviews and mock interview guidance.

5. Data Security

We apply robust administrative and technical protections, including:

  • Encrypted data in transit via TLS / HTTPS protocols.
  • Encrypted password hashing and token-based session management.
  • Role-based access controls to prevent unauthorized data exposure.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your information:

  • Review or update your personal and academic profile at any time.
  • Request deletion of uploaded resumes and account data upon graduation.
